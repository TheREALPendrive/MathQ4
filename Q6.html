Step-by-Step Solution
Step 1: Find Slope of Line 1
4x − 2y = 7 → y = 2x − 7⁄2
Slope (m1) = 2
Step 2: Find Slope of Line 2
−2x + 4y = 9 → y = 1⁄2x + 9⁄4
Slope (m2) = 1⁄2
Step 3: Compare the Slopes
Parallel lines must have identical slopes. Since 2 ≠ 1⁄2, the slopes are not equal.
Final Conclusion
The lines are not parallel because they do not have equal slopes.
