Parallel vs. Perpendicular Lines

Parallel lines never intersect and always have the exact same slope.

Perpendicular lines intersect to form ninety degree angles. The relationship between perpendicular slopes is that they are negative reciprocals of each other, meaning if you multiply them together the result is negative one.
